# HighScoop news and education workflow

HighScoop keeps its recruitment workflow and adds `뉴스` as a separate editorial category. News collection uses ordinary news/RSS queries, article kinds and issue discovery. Recruitment sources and filters remain active for recruitment categories. An explicit news collection excludes official recruitment boards. A full-channel collection processes each keyword in its own mode.

News articles default to the eight-page `set:n03` workflow, with source analysis, evidence references, education connections, questions, caption, optional verified resources and HighScoop's common final page. Explicit template choices and existing saved decks retain their format. Manual eight-page authoring is also available under HighScoop. The selected source-analysis provider still applies to the admitted family execution plan.

`consolidateHighscoopNews` runs once at startup after the earlier science/news consolidation. It preserves every item, revision, media record, source link, request receipt and publication identity. Source items and keywords move into HighScoop/news; archived channel rows remain for historical references. Directory metadata supplies old channel IDs and keys, allowing saved channel selections to resolve to HighScoop without hardcoded database IDs.

Immutable evidence rows and their payloads remain unchanged. Read access follows only explicit channel redirects and continues to require the same owned item. Other owned editing records move with their items. Current Canva Studio binding versions advance with the unchanged source revision and moved card, while stale bindings remain stale. Retired Canva masters remain archived for their existing jobs and are not selected for new HighScoop work.

Old publication destinations are disabled. Pending old-account deliveries are cancelled and active approvals require fresh account review; completed publication records and destination snapshots remain intact. Running work prevents the ownership migration. Collection writes lock and recheck their owner so a retired channel cannot receive late results.

The migration retains original classifications and target collection settings in an internal backup setting, and records an idempotent completion marker. It does not generate content, send posts, edit Canva originals, copy credentials, or enable paid auto-drafting. Existing discovery preferences are carried over when HighScoop has no explicit discovery settings.

Validation includes duplicate primary sources, immutable evidence, manual-request replay, old URLs, Canva binding versions, account separation, rollback during active work, non-recruitment news collection, and actual full-deck generation with mocked providers. The built-app browser check covers the removed legacy tab, saved channel selection, news filters and explicit news collection at desktop and mobile widths.
